Professional Summary

I am a PhD Candidate (ABD) in Electrical Engineering at UCF, with over a decade of university-level teaching, research, and student project supervision in Bangladesh and the US.

My current doctoral research focuses on intelligent communication and sensor network systems, including free-space optical (FSO) and visible light communication (VLC) technologies, with applications in AI-assisted optimization and UAV-enabled platforms. I also have expertise in antenna design, renewable energy, and robotics.

Research

Research Areas & Projects

Current and past research directions in the Networking and Wireless Systems Lab at UCF (NSF-funded).

Free-Space Optical Communication

Modeling and optimization of FSO links for mobile platforms, including atmospheric attenuation, beam propagation, pointing error, vibration effects, and multi-element transceiver layout design for in-band full-duplex UAV links.

Visible Light Communication

Optimization of LED power allocation, LED and IoT device clustering, and communication fairness under strict illumination and uniformity constraints in dense indoor IoT environments using multi-element hemispherical bulbs.

System-Level Optimization

Mathematical and algorithmic frameworks for clustering, association, and resource allocation, with emphasis on scalable, hardware-aware wireless system design and AI-assisted network optimization.

Active Projects

Aug 2022 – Present

3D RF/FSO Mesh Networking with Challenged Infrastructure

Designing multi-element transceiver systems for FSO communication between UAVs. Conference paper published; journal paper under review; hardware implementation ongoing.

Aug 2024 – Present

Clustering Optimization in VLC for IoT Networks

VLC-based architecture for scalable communications to IoT devices using a multi-element hemispherical bulb. Journal paper under 2nd phase review.

Jan 2025 – Present

UAV Vibration Study

Experimental analysis of UAV-induced vibrations on optical wireless link stability and receiver design optimization. Conference paper in progress.
